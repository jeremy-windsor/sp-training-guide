## CRITICAL Findings

### C1. FEC BER Thresholds Wrong for Modern Coherent Optics
- Stated thresholds only apply to legacy HD-FEC (10G/40G)
- Modern SD-FEC (100G/200G/400G) corrects to ~2×10⁻² to 3.8×10⁻², not 10⁻⁵
- Thresholds off by three orders of magnitude for dominant FEC type
- Would cause engineers to panic on healthy links or miss genuine degradation
- **FIX APPLIED:** Added explicit HD-FEC vs SD-FEC thresholds with Q-factor guidance

### C2. Missing CoPP/Control Plane Protection Discussion
- Module discusses control plane storms extensively but never mentions CoPP
- CoPP is THE primary defense against cascade amplification
- **FIX APPLIED:** Added CoPP to cascade prevention checklist
